# Phase 5 dynamic record, public getters, and kinematic transition

`NxPhysicsActorDynamicsTests` creates an explicit-mass box and a density-computed box through each staged DLL. The pinned oracle is `NxPhysics.dll` SHA-256 `4b7db3e126735c576f79fe5666e6fa661de9724b2a78808bb0924325ac79602c`. The explicit `NxBodyDesc` supplies mass 5, diagonal inertia (2, 3, 4), linear/angular velocities (1, 2, 3)/(4, 5, 6), damping 0.2/0.3, and default body flags. The descriptor loader at RVA `0x1a350` writes record offsets `0x188` (mass), `0x18c..0x194` (inertia), `0xc0..0xcc` (reciprocals), `0xb8/0xbc` (damping), and `0x6c..0x80` (velocities), and the candidate now initializes those observed fields. Public actor getters read them under the Scene read guard: `getMass` slot 36/RVA `0x25c0`, damping slots 42/44 (`0x2610`, `0x2680`), and velocity slots 47/48 (`0x33b0`, `0x3440`).

The second actor has one identity-pose box with radii (1, 2, 3), density 2, and zero descriptor mass/inertia. The oracle computes mass 96 and diagonal inertia (416, 320, 160), which match `8*density*x*y*z` and `mass/3` times the sums of squared other radii. The candidate now reconstructs this measured one-box path and its reciprocal words. Non-box and compound geometry, rotated or translated shapes, zero-inertia explicit-mass cases, and floating-point edge conditions still require the full mass-property implementation.

RVA `0x19620` is the kinematic transition called by actor body-flag slots 78/79 when mask bit `0x80` is set. In the measured unshared record, raising that flag zeros all four inverse mass/inertia words and allocates a 0x20-byte state block at record `+0x118`; clearing restores the reciprocals and frees the block. The record auxiliary manager's dirty mask becomes `0xb0000` when its entry starts at zero, and the actor is appended at active index one. The candidate matches these record, allocation, and manager observations. Shared-record/observer transitions, failure paths, contention reporting, and release while still kinematic remain open.

Thirteen exact lines are registered in Phase 5, raising the floor from 406 to 419. The staged oracle and candidate have `stdout_delta=0` across this target and the six earlier Phase 5 actor targets. Four aimed mutations were each rebuilt and run, then restored: zeroing record mass changed `dynamics mass` (`stdout_delta=6`); skipping the 0x20 allocation changed `dynamics kinematic` and allocation counts (`stdout_delta=4`); disabling one-box density computation changed `dynamics density_mass` (`stdout_delta=4`); and dropping the extra `0x10000/0x20000` dirty bits changed `dynamics kinematic_dirty` from `b0000.2.1` to `80000.2.1` (`stdout_delta=2`).
