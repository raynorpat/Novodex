# Phase 5 public actor body flags and lock dependency

`NxPhysicsActorBodyFlagTests` loads the pinned oracle and CMake-built candidate in separate processes and creates a dynamic box actor with the default `NxBodyDesc`. The pinned DLL has SHA-256 `4b7db3e126735c576f79fe5666e6fa661de9724b2a78808bb0924325ac79602c`. Its concrete actor table at image RVA `0x104530` puts `raiseBodyFlag`, `clearBodyFlag`, and `readBodyFlag` in slots 78–80 (RVAs `0x84d0`, `0x8670`, `0x2c90`). The setters modify the dynamic record's 32-bit flags at `+0x10c` and mark its Scene auxiliary entry at `+0x40` with `0x80000`; the reader tests the **full word** under the read guard. Descriptor flags initialize that record. The record's `+0x120` points to the Scene auxiliary manager.

The public probe exposed a dependency missed by older synthetic object-layout checks: `NpScene` allocated 0x20-byte lock blocks but left the `CRITICAL_SECTION` uninitialized. Connecting the actor reader initially crashed the candidate. The oracle's lock constructor at RVA `0x5b6a0` calls `InitializeCriticalSection`; the candidate now initializes and destroys those sections and uses the same guard protocol for public actor flag/group mutation. This also removes the prior actor metadata virtuals' unguarded direct access. The earlier synthetic readBodyFlag fixture used low-bit masks only and therefore missed that the candidate read one byte instead of the oracle's dword. The public wrappers keep their lock primitive local so the repository's direct-link proof targets still link without `ObjectModel.cpp`.

The oracle and candidate match on ten registered lines covering creation, record/manager link, two lock links, default visualization flag, raising and clearing body flags, direct record words, allocation neutrality, and requeue after clearing an auxiliary flag. The requeue drive changes the manager entry from zero to `0x80000`, appends record ID zero as active index one, and restores the synthetic manager state before release. The Phase 5 assertion floor rises from 396 to 406; the other five staged Phase 5 actor targets remain exact. The Phase 5 gate evaluates all 406 assertions and exits on its pre-existing explicit final-vtable marker (`layout candidate mismatches=1`).

Two aimed mutations were rebuilt and run, then restored. Narrowing the reader back to a byte changed `bodyflag_descriptor` from `1.0.100` to `0.0.100` (`stdout_delta=4`). Suppressing the manager dirty bit changed `bodyflag_requeued` from `80000.2.0.1` to `0.2.0.1` (`stdout_delta=2`). The restored control has `stdout_delta=0`. The setters' special `NX_BF_KINEMATIC` transition at RVA `0x19620`, capacity growth under dirty queue pressure, lock contention, and static-actor warnings remain open.
