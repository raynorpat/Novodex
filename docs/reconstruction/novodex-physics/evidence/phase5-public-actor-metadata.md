# Phase 5 public actor metadata

The fresh-process `NxPhysicsActorMetadataTests` loads the pinned oracle and CMake-built candidate through `NxCreatePhysicsSDK`, creates a static box actor, and exercises actor group and flag virtuals. The pinned DLL has SHA-256 `4b7db3e126735c576f79fe5666e6fa661de9724b2a78808bb0924325ac79602c`. Ghidra places the concrete actor vtable at image RVA `0x104530`: slots 75–77 (`0x2ba0`, `0x2c00`, `0x2c60`) raise, clear, and read the flag word at body offset `0x14`; slots 85–86 (`0x35b0`, `0x3610`) write and read the 16-bit group at body offset `0x1c`. The descriptor loader at `0x2010` copies descriptor dword `0x0e` into the flags and the low 16 bits of dword `0x0f` into the group. The candidate now follows those observations. Public NxPhysics headers are unchanged.

The staged oracle and candidate transcripts agree on all six registered lines: `metadata_created=1`, `metadata_descriptor=7.1.2.7`, `metadata_collision_default=0`, `metadata_raised=9.1.1.3`, `metadata_cleared=1.0.1`, and `metadata_mutation_allocs=0.0`. These include descriptor propagation, default unset flag, public mutation and readback, direct body-word observations, and lack of allocation during mutation. All four previously registered Phase 5 staged actor targets remain exact. The assertion floor rises from 390 to 396.

Two aimed mutations were rebuilt and run against the oracle, then restored. Disabling `setGroup` changed `metadata_raised` from `9.1.1.3` to `7.1.1.3` (`stdout_delta=2`). Removing descriptor flag propagation changed `metadata_descriptor` from `7.1.2.7` to `7.0.0.7` (`stdout_delta=4`). The restored control has `stdout_delta=0`. Lock contention, error reporting, and other actor virtual slots remain open.
