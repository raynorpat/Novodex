# Fluid visualization (`phys_fn_003489`)

The candidate traverses the fluid manager and renders cached fluid bounds, particle-position crosses, velocity arrows, and kernel-radius circles. The particle branches implement inventory rows `phys_fn_003471` (`0x00084ff0`) and its continuation `phys_fn_003473` (`0x000852c0`); manager traversal and bounds belong to `phys_fn_003489`. IDA's body at RVA `0x00085800` shows these branches use the FluidModel particle stream (92-byte records), `FluidGetCellSize`, per-fluid visualization scale, and SDK visualization parameters.

The simulation fixture seeds one backend particle record in each of two licensed fluids through the existing `NxSetFluidPostStepParticle` fixture export. It enables all three particle visualization parameters, sets a nonzero per-fluid scale, and seeds cached bounds. The renderer counts lines by color and calculates an ordered endpoint fingerprint for each color. Oracle and candidate both report `fluids=2 bounds=2 streams=2 red-lines=34 green-lines=72 blue-lines=6`, with matching red/green/blue hashes `cdb7b47d/2999a5a5/18232b07`; full stdout and stderr match exactly. The fingerprint ignores four low mantissa bits to accommodate a few x87 circle-rounding bits, retaining coordinate differences above roughly `1e-5` at the fixture's scale.

Three candidate-only omission mutations were tested independently. Removing position crosses changes blue lines from 6 to 0; removing kernel circles changes green lines from 72 to 0; removing velocity arrows changes red lines from 34 to 24. Each mutation is rejected with `stdout_delta=2`. A wrong position scale preserves line counts but changes the blue endpoint fingerprint and is also rejected. Restored output matches exactly. Fresh Phase 5/6/7 gates pass at 2,881, 1,741, and 3,020 coverage assertions. Raw fixture, mutation, restored differential, and gate transcripts are archived under `evidence/raw/phase7-fluid-visualization-003489-20261017/`.

The initial particle-only probe left the row deferred because its endpoint fingerprint allowed a small rounding tolerance and it had not yet falsified the manager traversal itself. The later traversal mutation and restored control are recorded below.
